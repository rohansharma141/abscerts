## What ISO 37301 covers

ISO 37301:2021 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, evaluating, maintaining and improving an effective compliance management system. It gives an organisation a structured way to identify its compliance obligations, assign clear responsibilities, embed controls and monitoring, and demonstrate a genuine culture of compliance — moving compliance from a reactive, siloed activity to a managed, auditable capability.

Because ISO 37301 sets out **requirements** rather than guidance, a compliance management system can be **independently certified** against it — the certificate that customers, regulators and partners recognise. It replaced the withdrawn **ISO 19600:2014**, which offered only recommendations.

## Common questions

### What is ISO 37301 and how does it relate to ISO 19600?

ISO 37301:2021 is the international standard for a compliance management system. It replaced the withdrawn ISO 19600:2014 — where ISO 19600 offered guidance, ISO 37301 sets out auditable requirements, so a CMS can be independently certified. ABS helps you implement ISO 37301, and migrate an existing ISO 19600-based system where one is already in place.

### Does ABS certify our compliance management system?

No. ABS provides implementation and certification-readiness consulting. The certification audit and decision are carried out independently by an accredited certification body; we prepare your organisation and coordinate the engagement.
