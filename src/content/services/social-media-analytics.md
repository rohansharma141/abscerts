## What social media analytics is

Social media analytics is the practice of systematically analysing the data generated through social platforms, distilling meaningful insights from user interactions. It moves past vanity metrics like raw follower numbers to understand what genuinely engages an audience, how sentiment is shifting, and how different content and channels perform.

## How ABS approaches it

That means identifying the metrics that matter for your goals, analysing engagement and sentiment, and presenting findings clearly. - Measuring content and campaign performance
- Understanding audience engagement and sentiment

## Common questions

### What is social media analytics?

It is the systematic analysis of data generated through social platforms, distilling meaningful insights from user interactions — helping you understand what content resonates, how audiences engage, and how sentiment and reach change over time.

### What can social media analytics tell us?

Beyond follower counts, it reveals which content drives engagement, how audiences respond, emerging sentiment, and how performance compares across channels and campaigns — insight that guides marketing and broader business decisions.
