## What the service covers

ABS's vendor and supplier inspection provides independent verification across the supplier relationship — from assessing a supplier's capability to checking goods before they ship. It includes:

- **Factory Audits** — assessing a supplier's facilities, processes and capability
- **OEM Development** — supporting the development of original-equipment manufacturing
- **Raw Material Inspection** — verifying inputs before they enter production
- **Loading Supervision** — checking goods as they are loaded and dispatched

Because ABS's assessments are impartial, they provide a credible basis for verification for diverse organisations.

## Who it's for

The service suits organisations that source products from suppliers — particularly across distances or borders — where they need independent assurance that a supplier is capable and that what is shipped meets requirements. It is especially relevant to [manufacturing](/industries/manufacturing) supply chains.

## How ABS approaches it

ABS acts as an impartial third party: verifying at the points in the supply relationship where it matters most, and reporting objectively. For verification during and after production, it works alongside [Quality Assurance Inspection](/services/quality-assurance-inspection), and together they make up the [Third Party Inspection](/services/third-party-inspection) practice.

## Common questions

### What does vendor and supplier inspection cover?

Independent verification across the supplier relationship — factory audits, OEM development, raw material inspection before production, and loading supervision at dispatch — giving impartial assurance that suppliers and their output meet requirements.

### Why use an independent third party for supplier inspection?

An independent inspection gives a credible, impartial basis for verification that doesn't rely on the supplier's own word — most valuable when buyer and supplier are in different locations, or when receiving non-conforming goods is costly.
