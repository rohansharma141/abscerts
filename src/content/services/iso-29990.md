## What ISO 29990 covers

ISO 29990 was the international standard for learning services for non-formal education and training — covering how learning service providers design, deliver and manage their services to consistent quality. It gave training organisations a recognised way to demonstrate professionalism to learners and clients.

**Important:** ISO 29990 has been withdrawn and superseded by **ISO 21001:2018**, the management system standard for educational organisations. Most learning and training providers should now be working toward ISO 21001 — but organisations with established ISO 29990 systems still operate them, and ABS continues to support them.

## What ABS provides

For learning and training providers, ABS offers:

- **Support for existing ISO 29990 systems** for organisations that built one
- **A migration pathway to [ISO 21001](/services/iso-21001)** — mapping your current system to the successor standard and closing the gaps to achieve certification

This sits within the [ISO Certifications](/services/iso-certifications) portfolio and is closely related to ABS's broader training and education work.

## Timeline & process

Migrating from ISO 29990 to ISO 21001, or strengthening an existing learning-services system, typically takes around 10–14 weeks depending on maturity and scope. Each engagement begins with a fixed-price scoping call and a proposal within 24 hours.

## Common questions

### Is ISO 29990 still current?

No. ISO 29990 has been withdrawn, and [ISO 21001:2018](/services/iso-21001) — the management system standard for educational organisations — is now the recognised successor. ABS continues to support organisations with existing ISO 29990 systems and provides a migration pathway to ISO 21001.

### Should we move from ISO 29990 to ISO 21001?

For most training and education providers, yes. [ISO 21001](/services/iso-21001) is the current, certifiable standard for educational organisations and gives you a certificate recognised going forward. ABS can map your existing system and close the gaps to certification.
