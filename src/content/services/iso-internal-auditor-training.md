## What the course covers

ISO Internal Auditor training equips staff to plan, conduct, report and follow up internal audits of their own organisation's management system against an ISO standard. Every ISO management system standard requires internal audits, so this training gives organisations the in-house capability to meet that requirement and to find and fix issues before an external audit. It is delivered by **ABS Academy**, which has provided training services since 2004 with highly competent trainers.

## Who it's for

The course is aimed at employees who will run internal audits — quality, safety, security and compliance staff, and process owners — particularly in organisations that hold or are pursuing an ISO certification such as [ISO 9001](/services/iso-9001) or [ISO 27001](/services/iso-27001).

## Format

Internal Auditor courses are shorter and more focused than the [Lead Auditor](/services/iso-lead-auditor-training) programme, concentrating on the practical skills an internal auditor needs. For staff who only need to understand a standard rather than audit it, the [Awareness](/services/iso-awareness-training) course is the lighter option.

It sits within the [Management System Training](/services/management-system-training) portfolio.

## Common questions

### What is the difference between Internal Auditor and Lead Auditor training?

Internal Auditor training prepares staff to audit their own organisation's management system — a requirement of every ISO standard. [Lead Auditor](/services/iso-lead-auditor-training) training is more advanced and prepares people to lead full audits, including of other organisations.

### Who should attend Internal Auditor training?

Employees who will carry out internal audits — typically quality, safety, security or compliance staff and process owners — especially in organisations that hold or are working towards an ISO certification.
