## What survey management is

Survey management is the systematic administration, analysis and interpretation of surveys to gather insights from various stakeholders. Done well, it turns the views of customers, employees or the wider market into reliable evidence — not just a pile of responses, but clear findings you can act on.

## How ABS approaches it

ABS treats surveys as a research discipline: the questions are designed to avoid bias, responses are gathered and managed carefully, and the analysis draws out what genuinely matters. The emphasis is on credible, actionable insight. ABS brings a track record of delivering measurable results and cross-industry experience, with approaches that scale from startups to established enterprises.

## Common applications

- Customer satisfaction and experience surveys
- Employee engagement and feedback surveys
- Market and opinion research
- Post-engagement and stakeholder feedback

It is part of the [Data Analytics](/services/data-analytics) practice and its results are often tracked over time through [Dashboards](/services/dashboards).

## Common questions

### What does survey management involve?

It is the systematic administration, analysis and interpretation of surveys to gather insights from stakeholders — covering the full cycle from designing the survey to turning results into clear, actionable findings.

### Why use a structured approach to surveys?

A structured approach improves the quality and credibility of what you learn. Well-designed questions, sound sampling and careful analysis reduce bias and make findings trustworthy enough to act on.
