## What the service covers

ABS's HR consulting works across the whole people function. It includes:

- **HR documentation** — HR manual, policy and process development, benchmarked against industry and international standards
- **Organisation culture and training** — learning and development (training-needs identification, programme design, execution and effectiveness monitoring), executive coaching and leadership development, psychometric testing (such as MBTI, FIRO-B and DISC) for career progression, and change management including M&A
- **Performance management** — role and goal setting, performance and potential assessment, reward management and competency development
- **Employee health and wellness** — work-life balance, counselling and employee mental-health support, and employer branding
- **Compliance and audits** — HR process efficiency assessment and gap identification, process re-engineering, employment-law compliance, and DEI, CSR and ESG
- **Digitisation and benchmarking** — HR digitisation and automation, and market research

## Who it's for

The service suits organisations that want to strengthen their people function — building sound policy, raising performance, supporting employee wellbeing, and staying compliant — whether they have a mature HR team that needs specialist support or are formalising HR for the first time.

## How ABS approaches it

ABS combines hands-on HR delivery with an assessment-led mindset: understanding where the HR function stands, identifying gaps, and re-engineering processes so they are efficient and compliant. It complements [Recruitment Services](/services/recruitment-services) for the hiring and lifecycle side, and together they form the [HR Services](/services/hr-services) practice.

## Common questions

### What areas does ABS's HR consulting cover?

The people function end to end: HR documentation and policy; L&D and executive coaching; performance management; employee wellness; HR compliance and audits (including employment law, DEI, CSR and ESG); and HR digitisation — benchmarked against industry and international standards.

### Can ABS help assess and improve an existing HR function?

Yes. ABS offers HR process efficiency assessment and gap identification, process re-engineering and audit compliance, so you can see where your HR function stands and make it more effective and compliant.
