## Why PCI DSS matters for global businesses

Any organisation that stores, processes or transmits payment card data is expected to comply with the PCI Data Security Standard. For fintechs, payment processors, e-commerce businesses and SaaS platforms that touch card data, PCI DSS is contractual: acquiring banks and payment partners require evidence of compliance before — and throughout — the relationship.

The standard is recognised globally, so a single, well-scoped assessment supports your obligations across the markets you operate in. Demonstrating PCI DSS compliance also reassures enterprise customers that the part of your platform handling payments is independently assessed against a recognised baseline.

## What the assessment covers

PCI DSS is organised around a set of control requirements covering network security, protection of stored cardholder data, encryption in transit, vulnerability management, strong access control, continuous monitoring and a maintained information security policy. The right validation route depends on how you handle card data and your transaction volumes — ranging from a self-assessment questionnaire with independent support, through to a full on-site assessment for larger service providers.

Scoping is the most important step: reducing and segmenting the environment that touches cardholder data is usually the single biggest lever for lowering both risk and the cost of compliance. We begin with a readiness and scoping review so the formal assessment targets only what it needs to.

## Common questions

### Which validation level applies to us?
It depends on your role (merchant or service provider) and your annual card transaction volume. We confirm the correct route during the scoping review so you are not over-assessed.

### Can we reduce our PCI scope?
Almost always, yes. Tokenisation, outsourcing card capture to a compliant provider, and network segmentation can dramatically shrink the in-scope environment. We highlight these opportunities during readiness.

### Does PCI DSS overlap with ISO 27001 or SOC 2?
There is meaningful overlap in access control, monitoring and policy. If you already hold ISO 27001 or have a SOC 2 programme, much of that evidence supports your PCI work.
