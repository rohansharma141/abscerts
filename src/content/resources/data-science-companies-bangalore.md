Bangalore is home to a large concentration of data science companies. Here are five notable firms and the capabilities they offer.

## 1. Nanobi Data and Analytics Pvt Ltd

A cloud-inspired data and data analytics firm, Nanobi emphasises security features including delegated administration at multiple user levels. The company offers what it describes as a shared data platform that delivers unified perspectives through virtual information systems.

## 2. Dataval Analytics

Dataval focuses on delivering quality solutions in data analytics. Its portfolio spans Big Data, AI and machine learning, dashboarding, churn prediction, data warehousing, crop quality analysis, face recognition, object tracking and identification, yield prediction and more.

## 3. Impact Analytics

Described as one of the fastest-growing data science startups in Bangalore, Impact Analytics builds products centred on innovation and process automation. It provides SaaS solutions offering business visibility based on real-time, data-driven insights, with a focus on cost optimisation.

## 4. Crossroad ELF

Crossroad ELF positions itself as a provider of turnkey solutions for sustainable business growth, delivering customised solutions by integrating design, technology and innovation.

## 5. Indium Software

Offering digital engineering solutions, Indium's expertise spans application engineering, cloud engineering, data analytics, DevOps and digital assurance. The company maintains relationships with more than 100 clients across multiple regions and partners with ecosystem firms such as AWS and Mendix.
