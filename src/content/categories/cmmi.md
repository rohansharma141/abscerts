CMMi (Capability Maturity Model Integration) is a recognised benchmark for the maturity of an organisation's delivery processes — frequently a prerequisite for government, defence and large enterprise contracts. ABS supports the full path to a maturity level rating across CMMi for Development, Services and Suppliers: gap analysis, readiness work, and appraisal alongside a certified lead appraiser.
