When you source from suppliers and factories you don't control, an independent inspection at the point of production is the cheapest insurance you can buy. ABS provides third-party vendor, supplier and quality-assurance inspections that verify quality at source — catching defects before goods ship, when they are still easy and inexpensive to fix.
