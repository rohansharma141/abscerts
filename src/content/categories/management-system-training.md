## Why training and professional development matter

The strongest management systems are run by people who understand the standard from the inside — and the most credible audits are led by people trained to a recognised level. Management system training builds that competence, whether you are preparing to run internal audits, implement a new standard, or qualify as a lead auditor. For individuals, it is also a career investment: auditing and management-system skills are in steady demand across industries.

## What's included, and who needs it

Delivered through ABS ACADEMY, the training runs across four levels — Basic, Internal Auditor, Lead Implementer (advanced) and Lead Auditor — so professionals can join at the right point for their experience and goals. Courses span the major management system standards, including ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (occupational health and safety), ISO 22000 (food safety), ISO 50001 (energy), ISO 27001 (information security), ISO 22301 (business continuity) and ISO 31000 (risk management), as well as HACCP requirements.

The training is designed for professionals who want to demonstrate their competence and develop their careers in management-system auditing and related areas — from quality and compliance staff taking on internal-audit duties to those moving toward full lead-auditor roles.

## From training to a recognised credential

Training builds competence; personnel certification recognises it. For auditors and other specialists whose judgement organisations depend on, a recognised personnel credential is portable proof of competence that moves with them between employers and engagements, rather than relying on a CV alone. ## How ABS approaches training

ABS ACADEMY has delivered training since 2004, with an emphasis on practical learning under experienced tutors so participants can apply new knowledge immediately rather than absorb theory passively. Sessions are interactive, with participants actively involved throughout, and the academy maintains the impartiality and confidentiality that underpin credible certification and training alike.
