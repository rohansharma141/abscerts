Audit Preparation Guide

How to prepare your organisation and your team for a smooth certification audit.

Before the audit

  • Re-read the standard and confirm your scope
  • Close known gaps from your Stage 1 or gap analysis
  • Make sure records show the system operating over time

Documents to have ready

  • Policies, procedures and the Statement of Applicability
  • Internal audit and management review records
  • Risk assessment, objectives and performance data
  • Evidence for corrective actions and improvements

Preparing your team

  • Brief staff — they should know the policy and their role
  • Identify process owners who can walk the auditor through their work
  • Encourage honest answers; "we are working on it" is fine

During and after

  • Answer what is asked; show evidence rather than describe it
  • Treat findings as improvements, not failures
  • Agree corrective actions with realistic timeframes
  • Keep the system live for surveillance audits
