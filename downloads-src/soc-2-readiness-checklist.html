SOC 2 Readiness Checklist

Prepare for a SOC 2 examination against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria.

1. Scope & criteria

  • Confirm which Trust Services Criteria apply (Security is mandatory)
  • Decide Type I (point in time) or Type II (period of time)
  • Define the systems and boundaries in scope

2. Security (common criteria)

  • Documented information security policies
  • Risk assessment and risk management process
  • Security awareness training for staff

3. Access control

  • Role-based access and least privilege
  • Multi-factor authentication on key systems
  • Timely provisioning and de-provisioning
  • Periodic access reviews

4. Change & configuration management

  • Documented change-management process
  • Secure baseline configurations
  • Segregation of duties for deployments

5. Monitoring, logging & response

  • Centralised logging and monitoring (SIEM)
  • Alerting on suspicious activity
  • Documented incident-response plan and testing

6. Vendors & evidence

  • Third-party/vendor risk management
  • Backup, availability and recovery controls
  • Collect and organise evidence for the audit period
