Stage 1 Audit Readiness Checklist

What to have in place before your Stage 1 documentation and readiness review.

Documentation

  • Management system scope documented
  • Policy and key procedures in place
  • Statement of Applicability (for ISO 27001)

Planning & risk

  • Risk assessment completed and treatment planned
  • Objectives set with measurable targets
  • Legal, regulatory and other requirements identified

Evidence the system is operating

  • At least one internal audit completed
  • A management review held
  • Records showing processes in use

Logistics

  • Key personnel available for the audit
  • Access to sites/systems arranged (or remote access ready)
  • Nominated management-system contact
